List of available processes (v1.0.0)

For a more detailed description of each process and related references, please have a look at Section 4 of the NNLOJET manual. Each process below is available up to NNLO QCD.

  • pp→H+X
  • pp→H+1 jet

Note: For each process type contained at NNLO QCD, it is implicitly understood that NNLOJET also provides the NLO correction to the process with one additional jet and the LO of the process with two additional jets. For example, since Z+1 jet is available at NNLO, Z+2 jet can be computed at NLO and Z+3 jet at LO. To perform NLO and LO calculations of processes with additional jets in the final state, one can simply require a higher number of resolved jets in the runcard file. For example, the NLO correction to Z+2j can be computed via an NNLO calculation for the ZJ process requiring at least two jets.
